Formula Cheat Sheet

🏷️ Discount & Selling Price

Discount = (MP × Discount%) / 100

Selling Price (SP) = MP − Discount

*Discount is always calculated on Marked Price (MP).

📈 Profit & Loss

Profit = SP − CP (when SP > CP)

Loss = CP − SP (when CP > SP)

*Profit or Loss is the simple arithmetic difference.

📊 Profit% & Loss%

Profit% = (Profit / CP) × 100

Loss% = (Loss / CP) × 100

*Always calculated on Cost Price (CP)!

🏦 Simple Interest (SI)

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Total Amount = Principal + SI

*P: Principal, R: Rate % p.a., T: Time in years.
